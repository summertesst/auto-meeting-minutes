# Privacy and data handling

Auto Meeting Minutes reads text exposed in a local Microsoft Teams Live Captions
window and writes it to local files. It does not upload meeting data by itself.

Meeting transcripts, generated minutes, task audits, and generated website data
are runtime data and are excluded by `.gitignore`. Keep the `output/` directory
on a secured local drive and define retention/deletion practices that meet your
organization's requirements.

Before capturing, recording, summarizing, or sharing a meeting, obtain the
necessary participant consent and comply with your employer's policies, local
law, Microsoft Teams settings, and contractual obligations.
